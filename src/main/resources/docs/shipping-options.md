# Shipping Options

BrightCart offers three shipping speeds for US orders:

- **Standard (5–7 business days):** $4.95, or **free on orders over $50**.
- **Express (2–3 business days):** $12.95.
- **Overnight (next business day):** $24.95.

Orders placed before **2:00 PM Pacific Time** on business days ship the same day. Orders placed after the cutoff, on weekends, or on holidays ship the next business day.

PO boxes can only receive Standard shipping. Express and Overnight require a street address and a signature on delivery for orders over $200.

You can combine shipping: if some items are back-ordered, in-stock items ship first at no extra charge unless you choose "ship complete" at checkout.
