# Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or defective, report it within **14 days of delivery** for the fastest resolution. Take photos of the damage and of the packaging before discarding anything.

Start a claim from Order History by selecting "Report a Problem," choosing "Damaged or defective," and uploading your photos. Claims are reviewed within 1–2 business days.

Approved claims are resolved with your choice of a **free replacement** (shipped express at no charge) or a **full refund** including the original shipping cost. You do not need to return the damaged item unless support asks you to — if a return is needed, we email a prepaid label.

Damage caused by misuse, accidents after delivery, or normal wear and tear is not covered; those cases fall under the standard return policy instead.
