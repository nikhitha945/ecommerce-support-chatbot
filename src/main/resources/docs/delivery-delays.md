# Delivery Delays

If your package is past the estimated delivery date, first check the tracking status. "In transit" with no update for 3 business days usually means a carrier-side delay rather than a lost package.

Common causes of delays: severe weather, peak-season volume, address corrections, and customs holds on international orders. The tracking page usually names the cause under "Shipment Details."

What to do:

1. Wait until **7 business days past** the estimated date for domestic orders (14 for international) — most delayed packages arrive in that window.
2. If it still hasn't arrived, contact support with your order and tracking numbers.
3. If the carrier confirms the package is lost, we ship a replacement immediately or refund you — your choice.

If tracking shows "delivered" but you can't find the package, check with household members, neighbors, and around all entryways first; a large share of these turn up within 24 hours.
