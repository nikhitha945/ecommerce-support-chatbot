# Refund Timeline

Refunds are issued to your **original payment method** once your return passes warehouse inspection, which typically takes 2–3 business days after the package arrives.

After the refund is issued, allow **5–7 business days** for it to appear on your statement. Credit card refunds depend on your card issuer's processing cycle; PayPal refunds usually appear within 3–5 business days.

If you paid with a gift card, the refunded amount is added back to the same gift card as store credit.

Partial refunds apply when only some items from an order are returned, or when an item arrives back in a condition that reduces its resale value. Any deduction is itemized in the refund confirmation email.

If 10 business days have passed since your return was marked "received" and you still don't see the refund, contact support with your order number.
