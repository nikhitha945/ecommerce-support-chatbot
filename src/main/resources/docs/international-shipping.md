# International Shipping

BrightCart ships to more than 40 countries. International delivery takes **10–20 business days** depending on destination and customs processing.

Shipping cost is calculated at checkout by weight and destination zone. Import duties and taxes are estimated and collected at checkout for most countries, so there are no surprise fees on delivery. For a small number of destinations, duties are collected by the carrier instead — checkout will tell you which applies.

Some items cannot ship internationally due to carrier or regulatory restrictions, including lithium-battery products over 100Wh and aerosols. Restricted items are flagged on the product page and removed from your cart automatically if your address is international.

If a package is held in customs, the carrier will contact you directly using the phone number on the order — keep it accurate.
