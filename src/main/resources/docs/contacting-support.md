# Contacting Support

Reach BrightCart support three ways:

- **Live chat:** Mon–Fri 8 AM–8 PM PT, Sat 9 AM–5 PM PT. Average wait under 2 minutes.
- **Email:** support@brightcart.example.com — replies within 1 business day.
- **Phone:** 1-800-555-0142, same hours as live chat.

Before contacting us, have your **order number** ready (it starts with "BC-" and is in your confirmation email). For damaged-item claims, include photos; for refund questions, include the return tracking number if you have it.

The help center articles cover the most common questions — returns, refunds, tracking, and shipping — and are often faster than waiting for an agent. If your question isn't answered there, the chatbot on the support page can look up your order status directly.
