# Gift Cards

BrightCart gift cards are available in denominations from **$10 to $500** and are delivered by email, usually within minutes of purchase. They **never expire** and carry no fees.

Check your balance any time on the gift card balance page by entering the card number and PIN. The remaining balance applies automatically at checkout when you enter the card details on the payment step.

Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash, cannot be used to buy other gift cards, and are not replaced if lost — treat the email like cash. If you suspect fraud on a gift card, contact support immediately.

Refunds for orders paid with a gift card go back onto the same card as store credit.
