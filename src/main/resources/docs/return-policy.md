# Return Policy

BrightCart accepts returns within **30 days** of delivery for most items. To be eligible, items must be unused, in their original condition, and in the original packaging with all accessories and manuals included.

To start a return, log in to your account, go to Order History, and select "Request Return." You will receive a prepaid return shipping label by email. Drop the package at any carrier drop-off point listed on the label.

The following items are **final sale** and cannot be returned: gift cards, personalized or engraved items, and clearance items marked "Final Sale" at checkout.

Once the warehouse receives and inspects your return (usually 2–3 business days after arrival), your refund is issued automatically. See the refund timeline article for how long the money takes to reach you.
