# Payment Methods

BrightCart accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover, as well as PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. BrightCart gift cards can be applied on the payment step.

The billing address you enter must match the address your card issuer has on file, or the payment may be declined. If a payment fails, double-check the card number, expiry date, CVV, and billing ZIP before retrying.

We do not accept cash on delivery, wire transfers, or payment by check. Split payments are supported: you can combine a gift card with one credit card or PayPal account per order.

All payments are processed over encrypted connections and BrightCart never stores full card numbers on its servers.
