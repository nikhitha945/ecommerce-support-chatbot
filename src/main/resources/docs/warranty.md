# Warranty

Electronics sold by BrightCart include a **1-year limited warranty** covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.

The warranty does **not** cover accidental damage, water damage, unauthorized modifications, or normal wear such as battery capacity loss over time.

To file a claim, contact support with your order number and a description of the problem. If the issue is covered, you choose between a repair, a replacement, or a refund. Warranty replacements ship free and do not extend the original 1-year period.

Some brands include their own manufacturer warranty in the box — where both apply, you may use either, but a single issue can only be claimed once.
